# Verification report template

Adapt this to the task; a short paragraph is enough for a simple check. Replace
the descriptive cells with observations. This template does not require a file.

**Scope:** Task/acceptance, target directory, tested revision or files, and relevant
local changes. Distinguish task-owned changes from a combined worktree.

| Acceptance item | State | Observed evidence | Coverage or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Required observable result | Verified / Failed / Unverified / Approval needed / Not applicable | Actual command/tool, working directory, result, and retrievable reference | Relevant source, fixture, environment, freshness, or missing evidence |

**Conclusion:** What the observations establish, what remains unresolved, and the
next action if needed. Keep observed facts, inference, and owner acceptance distinct.
Do not claim all requirements verified while a necessary item remains failed,
unverified, or awaiting approval. Explain every Not applicable item.
